# Art Tinder deck (Manu, Nov 14): paintings, sculpture, installations and works on paper, from the
# Renaissance on, mostly 1850 and after. One card per line:
# id|name|by|year|country|at|kind|style|medium|tags|wiki|note
# year: ~ = circa. country = where the artist is from. at = where the work is (museum, city).
# kind: painting, sculpture, installation, paper (prints, drawings, cut-outs)
# tags: subject (f figurative, a abstract) + palette (c color, n muted) + feel (m calm, e intense)
# wiki = English Wikipedia title (used to find photos). No em dashes in copy.

## edition 1: the first day's ten
birth-of-venus|The Birth of Venus|Sandro Botticelli|~1485|Italy|Uffizi, Florence|painting|Early Renaissance|tempera on canvas|fcm|The Birth of Venus|Venus drifts ashore on a scallop shell, blown in by the west wind. Painted on canvas, still unusual in Florence then, it was one of the first big mythological nudes since antiquity.
pearl-earring|Girl with a Pearl Earring|Johannes Vermeer|~1665|Netherlands|Mauritshuis, The Hague|painting|Dutch Golden Age|oil on canvas|fcm|Girl with a Pearl Earring|Not a portrait but a tronie, a Dutch study of a face, here in an exotic turban. The blue is ultramarine, ground from lapis lazuli and pricier than gold.
great-wave|The Great Wave off Kanagawa|Katsushika Hokusai|~1831|Japan|The Met, New York (one of many impressions)|paper|Ukiyo-e|woodblock print, ink and color on paper|fce|The Great Wave off Kanagawa|From the series Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji, printed in the thousands. The deep blue is Prussian blue, then a new import to Japan. Fuji sits tiny in the trough of the wave.
starry-night|The Starry Night|Vincent van Gogh|1889|Netherlands|MoMA, New York|painting|Post-Impressionism|oil on canvas|fce|The Starry Night|The view from his window at the asylum in Saint-Rémy just before sunrise, with an invented village and a huge cypress. He painted it from memory, during the day.
the-kiss|The Kiss|Gustav Klimt|1908|Austria|Belvedere, Vienna|painting|Vienna Secession|oil and gold leaf on canvas|fce|The Kiss (Klimt)|The peak of his Golden Period: two lovers wrapped in one robe, his side covered in rectangles, hers in circles. The Austrian state bought it right off its first show in 1908.
unique-forms|Unique Forms of Continuity in Space|Umberto Boccioni|1913|Italy|The Met, New York|sculpture|Futurism|bronze|fne|Unique Forms of Continuity in Space|A striding figure blurred by speed, the Futurist dream of motion in solid form. Boccioni made it in plaster; the bronzes were cast after he died in 1916. It's on Italy's 20 cent coin.
two-fridas|The Two Fridas|Frida Kahlo|1939|Mexico|Museo de Arte Moderno, Mexico City|painting|Mexican modernism|oil on canvas|fce|The Two Fridas|Painted the year she divorced Diego Rivera: one Frida in a white European dress, one in Tehuana dress, their exposed hearts joined by a single vein. Her largest painting.
rothko-61|No. 61 (Rust and Blue)|Mark Rothko|1953|United States|MOCA, Los Angeles|painting|Color field|oil on canvas|acm|No. 61 (Rust and Blue)|Soft-edged blocks of color floating on a field, meant to be seen up close so they fill your sight. Rothko said people who wept in front of his paintings were having the same experience he had painting them.
souls-of-millions|Infinity Mirrored Room: The Souls of Millions of Light Years Away|Yayoi Kusama|2013|Japan|The Broad, Los Angeles|installation|Contemporary|mirrors, LED lights, water|acm|Infinity Mirror Room|A small dark room of mirrors and black water hung with hundreds of tiny LED lights that fade in and out, so it seems to go on forever. Visitors get about a minute inside.
maman|Maman|Louise Bourgeois|1999|France|Guggenheim Bilbao (one of six casts)|sculpture|Contemporary|bronze, stainless steel, marble|fne|Maman (sculpture)|A spider more than 9 m tall, an ode to her mother, who repaired tapestries. Under its body hangs a sac of marble eggs.
